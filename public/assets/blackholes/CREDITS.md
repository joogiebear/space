# Black hole images

Two real images, both by the Event Horizon Telescope (EHT) Collaboration, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/),
from Wikimedia Commons, saved as 960 px JPEGs. Credited in the app on each picture's info panel. The colours are false:
the pictures are made from radio waves.

| File | Object | Commons file | Credit | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `m87.jpg` | Messier 87* (released 10 April 2019) | `Black hole - Messier 87 crop max res.jpg` | EHT Collaboration | CC BY 4.0 |
| `sgra.jpg` | Sagittarius A* (released 12 May 2022) | `Sag A*.jpg` | EHT Collaboration | CC BY 4.0 |

Every other picture in the Black Holes view is drawn by a shader (`src/scenes/blackholes/shaders.ts`), not a photograph.
